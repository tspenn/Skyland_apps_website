# Welcome to My$

([My-Mny.com](https://my-mny.com/))

Ever look at your bank balance and think, *“Can I actually afford this right now?”*

Your bank shows you what you have today — but it has no idea your rent is due Thursday, your car payment hits Friday, and your phone bill auto-pays on the weekend. That balance? It’s a lie.

My$ fixes that.

With one tap, My$ shows you your True Available Number — what you can actually spend right now, after every bill, after your safety buffer, before your next paycheck. No math. No spreadsheets. No surprises.

No Manual Tracking: My$ syncs your cash flow patterns automatically. No typing, no logging, no chores.

Bonus Tip Calculator: Out at dinner? Split the bill and figure out your share in seconds.

## It’s Not Magic — It’s Math

My$ connects to your bank through Plaid — the same secure network used by Venmo, Acorns, and thousands of other tools. Your bank password never touches us.

From there, My$ identifies your regular income and recurring bills automatically. It knows your rent hits on the 1st. It knows your paycheck lands on Friday. It does the subtraction.

The result is one number — what you can actually spend right now, without derailing next week.
