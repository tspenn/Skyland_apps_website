## What it is — and is not

It is a private messaging and file exchange system for the information you do not want sitting in email, consumer clouds, or the open internet.

It is not a full replacement for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, or everyday email. Those tools still work well for normal communication. My Lokr exists for the things that need tighter control.

## Where the privacy actually comes from

Your messages and files stay in a private system. They are not sent through Google, Microsoft, Apple, Samsung, or consumer email networks. People only see the conversations they are invited into. Attachments live in locked storage with short-lived download links. Free has no ads and no ad trackers. Stripe is used only if you pay — and only for billing, never for your conversations or Vault files.
