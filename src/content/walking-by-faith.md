# Walking By Faith

A Bible tool made for reading.

Read and listen — in the browser, on your phone, at your fingertips.

**[Start at go-bible.com](https://www.go-bible.com/)**

---

Walking By Faith is a Bible tool for people who want the text close, easy to read, and easy to hear. Open a passage. Listen along. Keep going when your hands are busy.

It is web based on purpose. Open it in the browser. Add it to your home screen. Same tool on your phone, tablet, and laptop. No store listing. No bloat.

---

## What you get

* Reading that stays out of the way so the page is the point.
* Listening when you want the words in your ear, not only on the screen.
* A light companion you can open and close — nothing sitting on the device when you are done.
