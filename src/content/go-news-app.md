### Search — find it now

Looking for something specific? Search across the whole feed instantly. Track a breaking story. Catch what you missed. No scavenger hunt.

Open a story and Go News brings you the full article in a clean reader — not a maze of pop-ups, share bars, and “you might also like.” Stay briefed in seconds, not hours.

Your personal intelligence briefing. Bookmark any article. File it into named folders — “Faith,” “Africa Watch,” “Business,” “Sunday briefing,” whatever you need. Go News stores the full text when you save, so the story is yours even after the original page changes or disappears.

Saved it on Wi-Fi? Read it anywhere. On a plane. Underground. Out of signal. Look for the Archived badge — that story is ready, no connection required. Turn on local download on each device you want to use offline.

### At your fingertips on all your devices

Sign in on your phone, your tablet, your laptop. Go News picks up where you left off. Your topic filters, saved articles, and folders follow you. Cloud sync is included with Basic, Pro, and Premium. One account. All your devices. Always web based.

Traditional news readers build an advertising profile from every headline you touch. Go News does not. We do not track your reading to sell a version of you. We do not sell your data. We do not share it. Your reading is private, stored securely, and yours.
