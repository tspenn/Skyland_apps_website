## What is LnkLokr?

LnkLokr is your personal content vault. Quickly save links, images, and text from your clipboard into organized categories. No sign-up required, everything stays on your device.

---

## Quick Start

1. **Visit lnklokr.com** —
2. **Add to Home Screen** — Install as an app for the best experience
3. **Copy → Open → Tap** — Copy any link, open LnkLokr, tap a category. Done!

---

## Categories

**Keep** (Gold) — Your quick-access file storage. Perfect for links, images, and content you reference often. *Examples: Favorite recipes, Work resources, Reference images, Important documents*

**Borrow** (Purple) — Temporary items and shopping lists. Great for things you need temporarily or want to track. *Examples: Shopping lists, Wish lists, Items to return, Temporary references*

**Share** (Tomato) — Content you want to send to others. Tap items here to share via your device’s native sharing. *Examples: Links to share, Images for friends, Recommendations, Gift ideas*

**Bury** (Sea Green) — Private, PIN-protected storage. For sensitive content you want to keep secure. *Examples: Private documents, Personal notes, Sensitive images, Confidential links*

**Appearance & Themes**

LnkLokr offers three visual themes to match your style and environment:

* **Light Mode** — Bright, colorful pastels with the full treasure chest aesthetic
* **Dark Mode** — Easy on the eyes for nighttime browsing
* **Office Mode** — A discreet grayscale design that blends into professional environments

To change themes: Open **Settings** → scroll to **Appearance** → tap your preferred theme.

**Pro Tip:** Use Office Mode when you want to check your saved items at work without the playful colors drawing attention!

## Tips & Tricks

**Clipboard Workflow** — Copy any link, image, or text, then open LnkLokr and tap a category to instantly save it.

**Office Mode** — Need a discreet look? Switch to Office mode in Settings for a professional grayscale theme.

**iPhone & iPad** — Open LnkLokr in Safari → Tap the Share button (box with arrow) → “Add to Home Screen”. Use the Share sheet in any app to save content!

**Android** — Open in Chrome → Tap the menu (⋮) → “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen”. Share content directly from any app to LnkLokr!

**Desktop Chrome** — Install the LnkLokr Chrome extension to right-click and save pages, links, and images directly from your browser.

**Desktop PWA** — In Chrome/Edge, click the install icon in the address bar to add LnkLokr as a desktop app.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I save something?** Copy content to your clipboard (link, image, or text), open LnkLokr, and tap one of the four category buttons. The item will be saved automatically!

**What’s the difference between Keep and Borrow?** Keep is for permanent, frequently-accessed items. Borrow is for temporary items like shopping lists or things you need to track short-term.

**How does the PIN work for Bury?** The first time you access Bury, you’ll set a 4-digit PIN. This PIN protects all items in Bury. The PIN is stored securely on your device.

**Is my data stored in the cloud?** No, all data is stored locally on your device. Your items never leave your phone or browser.

**Can I move items between categories?** Yes! Tap any item to open its details, then use the move buttons to relocate it to a different category.

**What is LokBx?** LokBx is a companion feature for managing larger collections with advanced filtering, sorting, and organization tools. Access it from the main menu.
