## How an operation runs

**Deploy an operative.** Pick the signal — equity, crypto, news keyword, competitor page, industry feed, public price, weather, seismic, air quality — set the threshold, give it a portfolio. Under a minute.

It watches whether your laptop is open or not. Hourly checks. No polling on your end.

When the condition fires, you get a push on the device. Agency clearance adds a Sunday-night digest of everything that happened across the desk. Notifications must be on.

## What GIA watches

Equities. Digital assets. News and keywords. Sports. Public pages — pricing pages, filings, anything on an open URL. Industry RSS. Retail and market prices. Weather. Seismic. Air quality.

You are not asking GIA to hold your bank account. That is My$ and MNY$. GIA is signals — the things that move a decision before the report exists.

## Portfolios, not a pile of alerts

A single watch is a hobby. A portfolio is a desk. Group operatives the way you actually think: a competitor set, a ticker book, a region, a launch. Director clearance holds four portfolios. Agency clearance does not cap the operation.

## Who this is for

Founders who need to know when a competitor moved. Operators who cannot refresh ten tabs. Traders and consultants who want the ping, not the platform. People making decisions with information — not waiting for a quarterly report.

If you only need one or two personal watches — a sale, a storm, a stock — that is [My Secret Agent](/my-secret-agent). GIA is the operation: more coverage, a command view, portfolios, and a briefing cadence built for work.
